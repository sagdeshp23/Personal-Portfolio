About Me

Most people use Power Platform to build forms. I use it to build systems people actually enjoy using.

Over the last 5+ years, I've been designing apps, automations, AI experiences, and business solutions that sit somewhere between engineering, product thinking, and "how did you even build this in low-code?"

My work lives across Power Apps, Power Automate, Dataverse, Power BI, Copilot Studio, AI integrations — and increasingly the space where automation stops feeling robotic and starts feeling intelligent.

What makes me different is that I don't think like a traditional developer. I care about flow, friction, adoption, UI details people don't consciously notice, the logic behind the logic, and the tiny things that make a system feel smooth instead of corporate.

I can discuss architecture with developers, process gaps with stakeholders, and then switch to presenting the same solution in German to DACH clients — without sounding like I memorised it five minutes earlier.

Somewhere between Power Fx, AI orchestration, and German theatre, I accidentally built a career around translating complexity into experiences that feel simple.
