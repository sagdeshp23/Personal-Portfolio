01About Me
Sagar

Most people use Power Platform to build forms. I use it to build systems people actually like using.

For the last six years I've built apps, automations and AI features that sit somewhere between engineering, product thinking, and "how did you even build this in low-code?"

Day to day that means Power Apps, Power Automate, Dataverse, Power BI and Copilot Studio. More and more, it means the AI parts that make an automation feel less robotic.

I studied economics, not computer science. So I think about flow, friction and adoption, the UI details people don't consciously notice, and the logic behind the logic.

I can discuss architecture with developers, process gaps with stakeholders, and then present the same solution in German to DACH clients.

Somewhere between Power Fx, AI orchestration and German theatre, I ended up with a career in making complicated things feel simple.
